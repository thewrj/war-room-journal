Why "The War Room Journal"?

Every campaign has a war room, the place where strategists set aside the speeches and study what actually happened, polling division by polling division. What they learn there stays private. This journal opens that room up.

Each week it takes one of Grenada's 15 constituencies and asks the questions a war room would ask: who won, by how much, what moved the vote, and what it means for next time. Sixteen volumes, from the Town of St. George to Carriacou and Petite Martinique, ending with a national verdict.

The analysis campaigns do behind closed doors, done in public, one constituency at a time.
