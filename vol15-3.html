Every Polling Station: The Sister Isles After Beryl

Hurricane Beryl flattened Carriacou and Petite Martinique in July 2024. Two years later the sister isles re-elected the NDC's Tevin Andrews by 976 votes over the NNP's deputy leader. The NNP carried one polling division, in its candidate's home village.

What can be done: a four-year plan for the sister isles

Carriacou and Petite Martinique needs an NNP that lives on the islands, not one that visits.

Year one, 2027: decide the candidate early, from the islands, and commit to that person for two elections. Whether Mr. Cox stays or a successor is chosen, the candidate should live on Carriacou or Petite Martinique and keep an office in Hillsborough open throughout the term.

Year two, 2028: run a public review of the Beryl recovery, island by island and village by village: which homes are rebuilt, which are not, what was promised and what was delivered. Publish it. That is the issue voters will judge in 2030/31, and the opposition should know it better than the government does.

Year three, 2029: recruit a captain for each of the 18 polling divisions, including both on Petite Martinique. Build from Dover, Windward and L'Esterre into Hillsborough, Bogles and Mount Pleasant.

Year four, 2030: target 489 switchers, starting with the 389 NNP voters lost since 2022.

The lesson for the whole series

Fifteen seats, fifteen stories, and the same lessons repeated. Candidates named early and kept in place hold up better. Long-serving members need planned, public successions. Parties that keep their elected members keep their seats. And the four years after an election decide the next one far more than the four weeks before it.

The NNP won one seat in 2026. The method that held St. George North West is not a secret. The question for 2030/31 is whether the party will apply it everywhere, starting now.

What it means for 2030/31

  1. Choose a candidate who lives on the islands and keep them there.
  2. Do not move candidates between seats: it lost both in this case.
  3. Name the caretaker early: 14 months was not enough after a 149-vote loss.
  4. Own the Beryl recovery record better than the government does.
  5. Keep an office open in Hillsborough throughout the term.
  6. Build from Dover, Windward and L'Esterre into Hillsborough.
  7. Captain all 18 divisions, including Petite Martinique.
  8. Target 489 switchers, starting with the 389 votes lost since 2022.
