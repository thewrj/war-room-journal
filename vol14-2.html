The Foreign Minister's Parish: How the NNP Fell Behind in St. Patrick West

Joseph Andall won St. Patrick West by 268 votes in 2022. Four years as Foreign Minister later, he won by 1,160. The NNP's share fell 13 points, faster than the national swing, and it did not carry one of 16 polling divisions.

The NNP's vote fell by 373, from 1,458 to 1,085. The register grew by 560, and 102 more ballots were cast. Turnout fell from 70.4 per cent to 64.8 per cent.

Unlike several seats where the NDC simply held its share while the NNP's support stayed home, here the NDC grew. Its gain of 519 votes was larger than the NNP's loss. Some of it came from new voters, and some, very likely, from NNP voters who switched.

Five hundred and eighty-one voters

To win the seat back, the NNP needs 581 voters to switch sides, or 1,161 more of its own supporters to turn out, nearly two-thirds of those who stayed home. Against a senior minister, that is not a realistic target for a single election.

But the seat was won by the NNP as recently as 2018, and it was close in 2022. Recovering the 2022 vote of 1,458 would cut the margin by about a third and put the party back in contention for the election after next.
