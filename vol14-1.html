The Foreign Minister's Parish: How the NNP Fell Behind in St. Patrick West

Joseph Andall won St. Patrick West by 268 votes in 2022. Four years as Foreign Minister later, he won by 1,160. The NNP's share fell 13 points, faster than the national swing, and it did not carry one of 16 polling divisions.

St. Patrick West, covering Sauteurs, Chantimelle, Mount Rich, Hermitage, Fond, Prospect and Samaritan, was won by the NDC's Joseph Andall in 2022 by 1,726 votes to 1,458 for the NNP's Victor Philip, a margin of 268. The NNP had held the seat through Anthony Boatswain until he stood down before that election.

"A senior minister who works his seat can turn a narrow win into a fortress in one term."Greg Hosten

On 5th November Mr. Andall, by then Grenada's Foreign Minister, won again by 2,245 votes to 1,085 for the NNP's Jelani Glean, with 53 for the DPM's Andy Best. His majority more than quadrupled.

A minister at home

Mr. Andall is one of the most senior figures in the NDC. He was the party's acting leader after the 2018 defeat, and since July 2022 he has been Minister of Foreign Affairs, Trade and Export Development. In 2026 he stood as a sitting Cabinet minister with four years in the seat.

His vote rose by 519, the largest increase of any NDC incumbent in the St. Patrick and St. Andrew seats. His share of the whole electorate rose from 37.0 per cent of registered voters to 42.9 per cent. A senior minister who also works his seat is the hardest kind of opponent there is.

A caretaker named in April 2025

The NNP named Mr. Glean as its caretaker for St. Patrick West in April 2025, alongside new caretakers for St. David, St. George South and St. Andrew South West. Like them, he had about 19 months before polling day.

In every one of those four seats the NNP's share of the two-party vote fell by around the national swing or more. In St. Patrick West it fell from 45.8 per cent to 32.6 per cent, a drop of 13.2 points, the third-largest fall in the country after St. Mark and St. George South.

Not one division

The NNP did not carry any of the 16 polling divisions. Its best results were at the Chantimelle R.C. School, with 41.3 per cent of the two-party vote across two divisions, and at the Hermitage S.D.A. Church, with 39.9 per cent. At Mount Rich it took 36.6 per cent.

Sauteurs and its surroundings went heavily to the NDC. At the St. Patrick's R.C. School the NNP took 19.1 per cent, 36 votes to 152. At the TAMCC campus, across three divisions, it took 26.0 per cent. At Samaritan, 22.0 per cent. At Prospect, 30.3 per cent.
