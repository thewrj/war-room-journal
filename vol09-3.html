The Rematch in St. George North East

Ron Redhead and Nimrod Ollivierre met for the second election running. In 2022 Mr. Redhead won by 1,087. In 2026 he won by 2,027, and the NNP did not carry a single one of 23 polling divisions. The NNP lost 585 votes, and 230 fewer people voted at all.

What can be done: a four-year plan for the north-east

St. George North East needs a reset more than a rerun.

Year one, 2027: decide the candidate early. If the party chooses a new face, name that person in 2027 and commit to two elections. If it keeps Mr. Ollivierre, give him a permanent office and a full-time organiser, and judge the effort by measurable growth each year. Open the office in Tempe, the largest polling site.

Year two, 2028: start where the NNP is closest, at Vendomme and Constantine, then build into Beaulieu and New Hampshire. Run a listening programme with young families in Tempe and Mount Parnassus, small businesses along the main roads, and residents of La Mode and Boca, and publish what they say.

Year three, 2029: recruit a captain for each of the 23 divisions, with a turnout target in each. The register grew by 890, and very few of the new names voted. Register and contact new voters every month.

Year four, 2030: set targets the party can meet and report against. Win the first polling division. Restore the 2022 vote of 2,133. Lift the NNP's share of the two-party vote above 40 per cent. A seat that cannot be won in one cycle must still be moving in the right direction.

The lesson for every NNP seat

Running the same candidate twice is a choice that must be earned. In St. Andrew South East the NDC's losing candidate came back stronger. In St. George North East the NNP's came back weaker, and the seat drifted further away.

Every NNP branch with a losing 2026 candidate should ask a simple question now: what will be different in four years? If the answer is not measurable growth in contacts, volunteers and votes, the rematch will end like this one.

What it means for 2030/31

  1. A rematch must be earned with four years of visible work.
  2. Judge a returning candidate by measurable growth each year.
  3. Watch turnout: it fell almost ten points, tied for the largest drop.
  4. Start at Vendomme and Constantine, where the NNP was closest.
  5. Open a permanent office in Tempe, the largest polling site.
  6. Register and contact the 890 new names on the roll.
  7. Captain all 23 divisions and win the first one.
  8. Restore the 2022 vote of 2,133 as the first milestone.
