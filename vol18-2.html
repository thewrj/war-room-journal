The Third Force: How the DPM Could Overtake the NNP

The Democratic People's Movement won 1,189 votes in its first election, 21,378 behind the NNP, and beat it in just one seat. New parties have risen quickly in Grenada before, but only after an older party had fallen apart. This volume maps the DPM's only realistic route to second place.

In the Town of St. George, the DPM won 730 votes to the NNP's 536. It finished ahead of the NNP in 10 of the 12 polling divisions, including all five at the National Stadium, which together gave it 454 votes; only in the two divisions at the Anglican Senior School did the NNP come second.

This was Mr. David's personal vote, built over twenty years as the Town's representative for two different parties. In 2022 he won the seat for the NNP with 1,329 votes; in 2026 he and the NNP's Troy Noel had 1,266 between them, of which Mr. David took more than half. The seat demonstrates that the DPM can outpoll the NNP where it fields a known and rooted candidate, and also how difficult that achievement is to replicate elsewhere.

It has happened before

Grenada's party system is younger and more fluid than it appears. The NNP itself was formed in August 1984 through a merger of smaller parties, and won the election that December. The NDC was founded on 18th October 1987 by George Brizan and Francis Alexis, in opposition to the NNP government, and within three years it was in office, having won 7 of the 15 seats in 1990 with 34.5 per cent of the vote.

The GULP, Sir Eric Gairy's party, dominated Grenadian politics for a generation; in 2026 it won 34 votes, all of them in St. John and St. George South East. Second place in Grenada is not a permanent possession.

The precedents, however, share a common thread. The NNP and the NDC both rose by bringing together established politicians, sitting or former members with local followings of their own, at a moment when an older party was divided or discredited. Neither grew one voter at a time from a single seat, and if the DPM is to become the second party, it will be by the same route.
